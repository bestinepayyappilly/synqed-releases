# Synqed — downloads

Your Android phone, on your Mac. Calls, messages, notifications, clipboard and
files move between your phone and your Mac over your own Wi-Fi — no cloud, no
account, no relay server.

**[Download the Mac app](https://github.com/bestinepayyappilly/synqed-releases/releases/latest)** · [synqed.studio](https://synqed.studio)

Synqed is two apps. This one is the Mac half; the Android half is on Google
Play. You need both, on the same network.

<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.synqed"><img src="https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png" alt="Get it on Google Play" height="80"></a>

## What it does

**Calls** — see who's calling in your Mac's notch, and answer, decline or reply with a message.

<img src="screenshots/calls.jpg" alt="Incoming call banner in the Mac's notch" width="720">

**Notifications** — every phone notification on your Mac, kept for 7 days and summarised on-device by Apple Intelligence.

<img src="screenshots/notifications.jpg" alt="Notification history with an Apple Intelligence summary" width="720">

**Clipboard** — copy on the Mac, paste on your phone, and the other way round.

<img src="screenshots/clipboard.jpg" alt="Text copied on the Mac appearing on the Android phone" width="720">

**Files** — drag a file onto the notch and it lands on your phone. Messages and contacts sync too.

<img src="screenshots/files.jpg" alt="Sending a file from the Mac to the phone" width="720">

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later
- An Android phone (Android 13 or later) with the Synqed app
- Both devices on the same Wi-Fi network

## Installing

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Synqed to Applications. Then install
Synqed on your phone from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.synqed),
open both on the same Wi-Fi and confirm the pairing code. The app is signed
with a Developer ID certificate and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a
Gatekeeper warning.

## Why this repo has no source

Synqed's source is private. This repo exists only to host release binaries,
because GitHub Releases is a better home for them than a web host: the files are
versioned, immutable, and free to download at any volume.
